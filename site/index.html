Free Nursing-Student Freebies Directory

Hand-checked, genuinely free resources for nursing students. Everything listed says exactly what it costs, what it requires, and when it was last verified. Curated by a nurse (Dylan Asdale, RN) in plain English - like a friend would.

Education only - never medical advice. Nothing in this directory is for clinical decision-making. For real patients, always follow your instructor, your facility's policy, and a licensed provider or pharmacist.

Flashcards

Free (or free-tier) ways to build and study card decks.

AnkiOpen source

A spaced-repetition flashcard program - the same technique med students swear by. Download shared nursing decks or build your own; you control everything on your own device.

  • What: Desktop + mobile spaced-repetition flashcards
  • Cost: Free on desktop, web, and Android ($24.99 one-time for the official iOS app, made by a separate developer)
  • Requirements: Download the app; no account needed. AnkiWeb sync is optional (free account)
  • Link: apps.ankiweb.net
Verified: Nov 2026 · no tracking beyond optional sync account

Quizlet (free tier)Free tier

The biggest shared-deck site: search millions of user-made nursing decks (pharm, med-surg, A&P) and study them in your browser or phone. Free tier covers creating and studying.

  • What: Web/mobile flashcards with huge shared deck library
  • Cost: Free tier (Quizlet Plus is paid - the free tier is enough for studying and card-making; some newer study modes are Plus-only)
  • Requirements: Free account for creating and to save decks
  • Link: quizlet.com
Verified: Nov 2026 · has ads on free tier

Brainscape (free web)Free tier

Flashcards that rate your confidence on each card and repeat weak spots sooner. Their free web plan lets you create unlimited cards and study shared classes.

  • What: Confidence-based flashcards on the web
  • Cost: Free web tier (Pro is paid: unlimited mobile access, images)
  • Requirements: Free account
  • Link: brainscape.com
Verified: Nov 2026

Cram.comNo-cost

A simpler, older flashcard site - search publics decks (there are plenty of pharmacology sets) and study without bells and whistles. Good when Quizlet feels like too much.

  • What: Simple web flashcards with shared decks
  • Cost: Free (ad-supported)
  • Requirements: None to study public decks; account to create/save
  • Link: cram.com
Verified: Nov 2026 · has ads

NCLEX-Style Practice

Question banks with rationales that cost nothing. Always study the rationale, not just the answer.

Khan Academy NCLEX-RN PrepNonprofit

Khan's free NCLEX-RN section: thousands of practice questions with rationales plus short video lessons on the high-yield systems. The most straightforward free prep out there.

  • What: NCLEX-RN-style questions with rationales + video lessons
  • Cost: Free, always (Khan is a nonprofit)
  • Requirements: None; optional free account to save progress
  • Link: khanacademy.org/test-prep/nclex-rn
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

RegisteredNursing.org Practice Questions

A large bank of free NCLEX-style questions organized by category (fundamentals, pharm, med-surg, psych, pediatrics, maternity) - each with a spelled-out rationale.

  • What: Categorized NCLEX-style question bank with rationales
  • Cost: Free on the site
  • Requirements: None to take practice questions
  • Link: registerednursing.org/nclex-practice-questions
Verified: Nov 2026 · site sells its own paid review course; the question bank itself is free

NursePlus (nurse.plus) Free Questions

Another free practice-question site with NCLEX-style item sets by topic and with rationales. A nice second source so you see questions written different ways.

  • What: Topic-based NCLEX-style practice sets with rationales
  • Cost: Free
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: nurse.plus/nclex-practice
Verified: Nov 2026 · has ads

NCSBN Official NCLEX Page

Not practice questions - the actual rulebook. The folks who write the NCLEX publish the current test plan, candidate guide, and how the exam works. Read it once so nothing on test day surprises you.

  • What: Official test plan, candidate handbook, exam info
  • Cost: Free PDFs (note: the exam itself has a registration fee)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: ncsbn.org/nclex
Verified: Nov 2026 · official regulator, no ads

Journals & Articles

Where to actually read research without hitting a paywall.

PubMed Central (PMC)NIH

A free archive of millions of full-text biomedical and life-science journal articles from the National Institutes of Health. When your care-plan requires an evidence source, start here.

  • What: Free full-text research article archive
  • Cost: Free, always (U.S. government)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

PubMed

The search engine for medical literature. Abstracts are free for everything; look for "Free PMC article" tags on results to get the whole paper. Pair PubMed searching with PMC reading.

  • What: Literature search engine (36M+ citations)
  • Cost: Free (U.S. government)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals)

A vetted directory of legitimate open-access journals, including many nursing titles. Handy when you need articles from a specific nursing journal and don't have a school library login.

  • What: Searchable directory of 20k+ open-access journals
  • Cost: Free
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: doaj.org
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

Scholarships

Real programs, no pay-to-apply sites. Re-check deadlines and eligibility every semester - they change.

HRSA Nurse Corps Scholarship

A federal scholarship: it can pay tuition, fees, and a monthly stipend in exchange for working a set period at a Critical Shortage Facility after you graduate. Serious commitment, serious funding.

  • What: Federal tuition scholarship + stipend for a service commitment
  • Cost: Free to apply
  • Requirements: U.S. citizen, enrolled or accepted in an approved RN program; annual application window; service obligation after graduation
  • Link: hrsa.gov/programs/nurse-corps-scholarship
Verified: Nov 2026 · deadline closes early in the year - check the site

AACN & Partner Scholarships

The American Association of Colleges of Nursing lists scholarships from its member foundations and partners (including the AfterCollege-AACN award). A good once-a-semester sweep.

  • What: List of nursing-student scholarship programs
  • Cost: Free to apply (varies by program)
  • Requirements: Varies by scholarship; typically enrolled in an AACN-member school or a nursing degree program
  • Link: aacnnursing.org/students/scholarships
Verified: Nov 2026

NSNA Foundation Scholarships

The National Student Nurses' Association's Foundation awards scholarships to pre-nursing and nursing students every year. If you're an NSNA member already, you've got a head start.

  • What: Annual scholarships from the student-nurse association
  • Cost: Free to apply
  • Requirements: Enrolled in a state-approved RN/LPN program (or pre-nursing); NSNA membership preferred; spring deadline
  • Link: nsna.org/foundation.html
Verified: Nov 2026

Drug References

Free, trustworthy references. Education only - for real patients, the licensed pharmacist and your facility's formulary win.

DailyMedNIH/FDA

The official FDA drug-label database. Every detail on the package insert - indications, contraindications, pregnancy category, more - free and authoritative. Great for pharm cards you can cite.

  • What: Official FDA drug labeling database (40k+ labels)
  • Cost: Free (U.S. government)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: dailymed.nlm.nih.gov
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

LactMedNIH

A free NIH BookShelf database on drug safety in breastfeeding - levels in milk, infant effects, alternatives. The reference many OB units quietly keep open.

  • What: Drugs and lactation reference database
  • Cost: Free (U.S. government)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/n/lactmedbook
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

Drugs.com (free tier)Free tier

Fast, readable monographs and a free interaction checker with a warning-tier system that's easy to understand. Consumer-oriented, but sourced from standard references.

  • What: Drug monographs, interaction checker, pill identifier
  • Cost: Free tier (Pro subscription adds more, not needed for study)
  • Requirements: None for most features
  • Link: drugs.com
Verified: Nov 2026 · has ads; always cross-check a second source for papers/care plans

Medscape

A deep professional reference: drug monographs with dosing, disease overviews, and free nursing CEUs with a free account. Clinician-grade, still $0.

  • What: Drug/disease reference + free CE courses
  • Cost: Free (requires account)
  • Requirements: Free account; full site behind a one-time login
  • Link: medscape.com
Verified: Nov 2026 · has ads and needs an account

Sim Labs & Scenario Learning

Free simulated-case experiences you can practice skills and judgment with - no manikin, no lab fee.

NHS e-Learning for HealthcareNonprofit

The UK National Health Service's free e-learning platform: hundreds of clinical modules (wound care, diabetes, mental health, safe medication) built as interactive case-style lessons.

  • What: Interactive clinical e-learning courses from the NHS
  • Cost: Free
  • Requirements: Free registration for most modules
  • Link: e-lfh.org.uk
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads; UK-context notes some care-standard differences

TRAIN Learning NetworkNonprofit

A national learning network (run by the Public Health Foundation) with thousands of free courses from CDC, HRSA, and state health departments - many case-based, some with certificates.

  • What: Public-health and clinical e-learning course catalog
  • Cost: Free
  • Requirements: Free account
  • Link: train.org
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

AHRQ PSNet Case Learning

The federal patient-safety network publishes real-world safety cases, primers, and weekly literature digests - an excellent (and free) way to learn why iPASS and SBAR exist.

  • What: Patient-safety cases, primers, and digest
  • Cost: Free (U.S. government)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: psnet.ahrq.gov
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads

Khan Academy Health & Medicine

Interactive A&P and clinical lessons - including walking through vital signs in their interactive vital-signs simulator-style tool. A gentler warm-up before lab day.

  • What: Interactive clinical and A&P lessons + tools
  • Cost: Free (nonprofit)
  • Requirements: None
  • Link: khanacademy.org/health-and-medicine
Verified: Nov 2026 · no ads
